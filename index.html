Privacy Policy

Welcome to CBT Sleep (hereinafter referred to as "the App"). The App is a 100% offline standalone sleep management tool based on Cognitive Behavioral Therapy for Insomnia (CBT-I) and scientific sleep interventions. We highly value your personal privacy and data security. This Privacy Policy is designed to explain how your data is treated and how you retain absolute control over your information.

Core Privacy Commitment: CBT Sleep is a purely offline, standalone application. We do not maintain any external servers, nor do we collect, upload, or store any of your personal information, sleep logs, audio preferences, or health data to any cloud service. All your data remains exclusively on your own local device.

1. What Data is Processed on Your Device

To provide you with structured sleep intervention courses (such as the 6-week program) and integrated sleep utilities, the App processes the following data entirely on your local device:

A. Sleep Diary & Health Metrics

Manually logged by you on a daily basis to generate localized actionable insights and track trends:

  • Time in Bed & Actual Sleep Time: Processed locally to calculate your real-time sleep efficiency.
  • Nighttime Awakenings: Including mid-night awakening counts and early awakenings (waking up before 5:00 AM).
  • Daytime Well-being Status: Your subjective evaluation of physical energy and mental focus for the day.

B. Program Progress & Tool Preferences

  • CBT-I Program Milestone: Tracks your unlocked chapters and completion status within the 6-week program (from Week 1 "Understanding Insomnia" to Week 6 "Relapse Prevention") locally.
  • Sleep Toolkit Usage Logs: Saves historical usage of specific relaxation methods and behavioral tasks such as Cognitive Shuffling, Progressive Muscle Relaxation (PMR), Paradoxical Intention Therapy, and the Stimulus Control Method.

C. 3-Stage Adaptive Binaural Beats Customization

  • Audio Brainwave Entrainment Settings: To optimize brainwave entrainment and soundscapes, the App tracks your interaction with the 3-Stage Adaptive Binaural Beats module locally. This customized algorithm adapts audio frequencies on your device to match your relaxation state without transferring any sound profiles or usage statistics outside your phone.

D. System Settings & Error Diagnostics

  • The App reads your local operating system configurations solely to adapt UI layouts (such as automatically dimming the display for Dark Mode at night) and does not request internet-based permissions such as GPS location, contacts, or photo gallery access.

2. How Your Information is Utilized

All algorithm operations, calculations, and frequency generations happen locally on your smartphone. The breakdown is as follows:

Data Type Localized Purpose
Sleep Diary Logs Calculates your "Real-time Sleep Efficiency" locally and dynamically updates personalized behavioral instructions ("Tonight's Prescription") and sleep restriction thresholds.
3-Stage Binaural Beats Executes real-time audio phase adjustments on your device to seamlessly guide brainwaves through progressive stages of deep relaxation.
Program Milestones Maintains continuous check-ins and locked/unlocked progress for the 6-week therapeutic sequence.

3. Data Storage and Retention

  • 100% Local Sandboxing: All logs, sleep diaries, sound entrainment parameters, and progress states are locked in your device's native application sandbox storage.
  • Zero Data Transmission: Because the App operates entirely serverless, your data is never broadcast over the internet. As developers, we have zero access to your files and cannot view, manipulate, or recover them.

4. Third-Party Sharing and Disclosures

Since we neither aggregate nor hold any of your information on external endpoints, there is strictly no possibility of sharing, renting, selling, or disclosing your health metrics to third parties (such as data brokers, insurance providers, or advertisers).

5. How to Delete Records & Purge Data

You hold complete ownership and physical control over your saved history. If you wish to delete your records or completely reset the App, you may do so through the following default operating system methods:

  1. Uninstalling the App (Recommended): Because all data is sandboxed, deleting/uninstalling "CBT Sleep" from your phone will trigger the operating system to automatically and permanently purge all local databases, sleep diaries, audio setups, and caches. This action is irreversible.
  2. Clearing Storage via System Settings: You can navigate to your phone's Settings -> Apps -> CBT Sleep -> Storage and select "Clear Data" or "Clear Cache". This immediately wipes out the local database, returning the App to a brand-new, factory-blank initialization state.

6. Children's Privacy

The App is completely safe as it does not harvest any online identifier data. However, we encourage individuals under 18 to utilize these behavioral techniques under proper guardian supervision.

7. Policy Changes

Should future iterations introduce cloud-based feature sets, data backups, or syncing infrastructure, an entirely revised policy will be provided with an explicit runtime consent prompt. Until then, the App will strictly adhere to this standalone, serverless framework.

8. Contact Us

For any queries regarding this standalone approach or device-side data handling, feel free to contact us via the support email listed on the app store.
